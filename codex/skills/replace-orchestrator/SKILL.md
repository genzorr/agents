# Replace Orchestrator

Transfer the ongoing role, not just the latest task. The existing orchestrator remains responsible until the successor understands and accepts the role, affected active owners have been told the new callback route, and the old orchestrator confirms the transfer. The successor inherits prior scoped user authority and outstanding obligations; the handoff does not expand them.

## Prepare

Use the accessible conversation and current authoritative project sources to build a focused handoff. Preserve the programme and motivation; user corrections, preferences and operating style; decisions and rationale; important failed approaches; open and user-owned choices; and source pointers with what each establishes. Separate user decisions and agreed work from observations, inferences, proposals and unknowns. Reconcile current state rather than treating an old summary as current.

Identify the current chat's title, project, working directory and identity; active owners, their assignments and writer custody; pending callbacks, dependencies and relevant routes; and the successor profile requested by the user. Preserve existing workers, their identities, settings, assignments and custody. Do not reset or recreate them, change their model or effort, or infer that idle means released. Record uncertain state as uncertain.

Discover the available Codex app or bridge tools for creating, reading, waiting on and messaging ordinary chats; use their actual schemas and route requirements. Verify the destination and authority for each message. A requested replacement authorizes creating the successor and the messages necessary for this transfer within the existing project scope; it does not authorize unrelated delegation, worktree creation, external effects or expanded work. If required controls or a usable route are unavailable, report the affected gap rather than guessing or silently changing the operation.

## Orient the successor

Create a fresh ordinary chat in the same project and working directory, with the same title and the user's requested creation profile. Use the supported creation controls; do not apply creation settings to existing chats. Give the successor the handoff, authoritative source locations, old-chat identity and route, current owners and pending callbacks, and the existing scope of authority. Ask it to orient itself and wait for the old orchestrator’s transfer acknowledgement before directing work, selectively investigating relevant segments of the old rollout, including compressed portions where accessible and authorized, to recover missing context. Respect applicable private-transcript access rules. If the user explicitly authorized delegation, the successor may use a read-only auditor with the requested model and effort, or configured defaults when unspecified; otherwise it investigates directly. Do not prescribe a model or create an auditor merely because the skill is in use.

Ask the successor to report its understanding of the programme, decisions, operating style, live work and uncertainties, and explicitly accept the role. Compare that report with the handoff and current evidence; correct material gaps and wait for acceptance. Reconcile material updates and callbacks that arrive during orientation with the successor and affected owners before transfer. Keep a single directing orchestrator during this interval.

## Transfer

After acceptance, notify affected active owners of the new callback destination using their authorized routes, then acknowledge the transfer to the successor and stop directing project work. Preserve each owner's existing assignment and settings; ordinary messages to existing chats omit model and effort fields, including nulls. Distinguish a message queued or accepted by transport from the owner's acknowledgement. Observe available replies and report any owner whose acknowledgement remains unobserved; do not claim receipt or completion from delivery alone.

Report the successor identity, acceptance, notified owners and any unacknowledged or unresolved transfer items to the user. Leave the old chat for the user to archive unless the user explicitly authorized archiving. If transfer cannot reach this point, retain the old orchestrator's responsibility and report the blocker and actual state.
