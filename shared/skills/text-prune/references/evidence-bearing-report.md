# Evidence-Bearing Report Mode

Preserve observations, sources, evidence and counterevidence, uncertainty, scope, non-claims, decisions, ownership, and next gates. Keep observation, conclusion, and decision distinct; shortening must not turn correlation into causation, partial coverage into completeness, or a proposal into an adopted decision.

When pruning changes a claim or its stated scope, open `docs/claim-discipline.md`. Retain provenance needed to verify the claim and report missing evidence as a limitation. Consolidate repeated evidence only into a canonical location that preserves every distinct source, condition, and contradiction.

The ledger must name any merged evidence units and the surviving authority. If a shorter statement would hide a live alternative or caveat, keep it or mark it `FLAG`.
