# Instructions and Prompts Mode

Preserve triggers, authority, boundaries, stop conditions, output contracts, completion criteria, non-obvious invariants, and progressive-disclosure links. Remove duplicated meaning, sediment, behavioral no-ops, and sprawl only after identifying the instruction owner and loading layer.

Open `docs/context-file-authoring.md` for always-on context and `docs/skill-authoring-principles.md` for skills or composition roles. Check exact-phrase assertions, source-shape and line-break contracts, imports, heading or anchor callers, catalog layers, traveling references, and validators before changing wording or structure. Never broaden authorization, trigger scope, or completion authority as a side effect of compression.

Installed `~/.codex` (runtime-home), `~/.claude` (runtime-home), and other installer-owned copies are not source targets. Refuse a requested edit there and identify the physically owning repository; do not infer ownership from an installed path or skill name.
