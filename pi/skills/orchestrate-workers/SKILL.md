# Orchestrate Workers

A helper for the current ordinary interactive Pi/T3 coordinator. Preserve its profile, authority and acceptance responsibility; this creates no separate ordinary owner. Use native leaves only for specific results the user has authorized you to delegate. The coordinator owns integration and evidence assessment. A worker report alone does not show that the work succeeded.

## Dispatch a bounded result

Before launch, read [Resolve the task and profiles](references/worker-contract.md#resolve-the-task-and-profiles) and [Decompose and dispatch](references/worker-contract.md#decompose-and-dispatch). Use qualified pi-subagents native tools, explicit fresh context and async receipts. Verify required permissions/inheritance rather than assuming them. Preserve the coordinator's settings and resolve only actual roles. Review remains with the coordinator unless the operator explicitly requests an independent reviewer; only then load [its protocol](references/independent-reviewer-protocol.md).

Give the leaf its assigned result, references, permitted actions, checks, stop limits and native return to its immediate parent. Keep one writer per shared resource and serialize conflicts. Workers remain leaves and may diagnose, repair and retest within their contract without an ACK after each correction. Preserve scientific/resource/privacy limits and useful failed evidence.

## Continue and assess

Read [Iterate, retain and verify](references/worker-contract.md#iterate-retain-and-verify) before steering, resuming, waiting on or consuming a result. Use exact admitted run receipts and supported native lifecycle controls; do not guess IDs, broaden permission or replace uncertain writers. Reuse applicable evidence and check missing, stale or integration-critical consequences. Repair ordinary failures within the assignment. If permission, resources or a required decision is missing, pause only work that depends on it.

Read [Return at the owning boundary](references/worker-contract.md#return-at-the-owning-boundary) before completion. Inspect the files and evidence needed to judge the result, and report whether each required assignment is complete, blocked, partial or failed. Report outcome, tested state, material gaps and next action through the existing conversation. Required independent review, when requested, remains required; a passing check cannot grant permission or complete an unresolved assignment.
